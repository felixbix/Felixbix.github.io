A little about me

Engineering mindset. Automation focus.

I’m Felix, a Chemical Engineering graduate building expertise in AI automation and intelligent workflow systems. I enjoy breaking complex processes into clear steps, connecting APIs and data sources, and designing systems that can recover gracefully when something goes wrong.

My current work combines n8n, PostgreSQL, JavaScript, APIs, and AI models. I’m especially interested in the space where automation engineering and project management meet: understanding a business problem, designing the system, building it, and communicating how it performs.
